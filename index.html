Olfactory Architecture

The Anatomy of an Extrait

Understanding how a true luxury perfume reveals itself in three harmonious movements over time.

Movement I

Top Notes (Head)

First 15 to 30 Minutes

The luminous overture. Made of volatile, delicate essences such as Calabrian bergamot, Tunisian neroli, and pink pepper. They create the unforgettable initial spark upon spraying.

Movement II

Heart Notes (Cœur)

Hours 2 through 6

The pulsating emotional core. Rich Damask roses, Tuscan leather, saffron threads, and warm iris butter bloom once top notes soften, providing full-bodied presence and nuance.

Movement III

Base Notes (Fond)

Hours 6 through 16+

The sensual, indelible dry-down. Aged Cambodian agarwood, Madagascar bourbon vanilla, warm fossilized amber, and ambergris linger on textiles and skin into the next morning.
